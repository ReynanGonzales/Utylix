PDF editor: safer saving, text directions, justified paragraphs keep their look, and clear messages when a PDF can't be opened; players stop the sound when closed

## PDF editor
- **A PDF that can't be opened now says why** (before, nothing happened when a file was not visible to Utylix, for example a server drive such as K: that a Utylix started as administrator cannot see). The reason is also written to `pdf-open.log` in `%APPDATA%\Utylix`. Any other open error shows a message too.
- **Text directions.** *Style ▾* in the Text tool: Normal, Turned right, Turned left, Upside down, Letters stacked, Mirrored, Backwards. Choose it before you click on the page (it stays for the next texts) or while typing. The Style button is now the first in the row.
- **Paragraph and Edit text keep the page's look.** Justified, centred and right-aligned paragraphs stay that way; the old words are hidden by the page itself, so watermarks, pictures and colours behind the text are no longer cut; one changed line of a justified paragraph stays justified.
- **Safer saving.** If the file was changed by someone else since you opened it (or moved or deleted), or the PDF is digitally signed, Utylix asks first: *Save as…*, *Replace it* or *Cancel*.
- **Redact is more thorough.** The words under a black box are also removed from bookmarks, comments, form fields and alt text.
- **Any letters in added text.** Chinese, emoji, Arabic, Hebrew, Thai and more are drawn as shapes, so they always show. Edit text warns before saving letters its font does not have.
- **Edit text on turned text** (pages with /Rotate and turned lines) now works.

## Players
- **The sound stops when you close the video or music player**, even when the playback engine is slow to answer.

## Other
- Right-clicking a scroll bar no longer shows Windows' light grey scroll menu on the dark theme.
