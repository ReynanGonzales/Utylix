PDF editor: tabs, blur, QR maker, background colours, clean paste and auto-save for safety; one-click small PDF copies in the right-click menu

## PDF editor
- **Tabs.** With two or more PDFs open, a strip of tabs shows on top: click one to switch (it comes up in the same place and size), the cross closes it, **+** opens another. Right-click a small page > **Copy the page(s) to** another open PDF. Copy and paste of things now also works between editor windows.
- **Blur** (Mark up tab). Drag over a name, a number or a face: soft blur, strong blur or blocks. Saving also takes out the words and pictures under it, so nobody can copy them out (can be turned off).
- **QR maker** (Add tab). A QR code for a web link, text, e-mail, phone number or Wi-Fi, with a live preview. Square or round look, a border, a picture (logo) in the middle, your colour. It is drawn as sharp shapes, not a picture; move it, resize it, double-click to change it.
- **Backgrounds.** *Edit text* has a **Background** colour for the words (saved under them). Pictures have **Remove background** (the same on-PC AI model as Background Remover) and **Background** (a colour behind the picture, which moves with it).
- **Paste as plain text.** Copy words from a web page, mail or chat, press Ctrl+V: they are cleaned (odd spaces, invisible characters, curly quotes, stray blank lines) and put on the page as normal text. Right-click offers *Paste the text here as one paragraph*.
- **Auto-save for safety.** While a PDF has changes you have not saved, Utylix keeps a copy of them every minute. If the program or the PC stops before you save, opening that PDF asks if you want them back. It never changes the PDF itself and is removed when you save or close.
- **Find and replace** is now one click away: a **Replace…** button in the Ctrl+F bar (or Ctrl+H).
- **Highlighter** colours (yellow, green, pink, orange, blue, purple) and **filters** in the Comments list (All, Notes, Highlights, Lines); marked words without a comment are listed too.

## Explorer right-click
- On a PDF: *Utylix Editor > Small copy for email (under 5 MB)*, *Small copy for chat (under 2 MB)* and *Very small copy (under 1 MB)*. They work in the background, save `name (small).pdf` next to the original and tell you the new size. The original is never changed.
