Signature from a picture works again, and setup no longer ticks the extra downloads

- **Sign > From a picture no longer comes out blank.** A signature saved as a PNG with a see-through background (the usual kind) was turned into an empty picture. Now it is used as it is; photos and scans on white paper are still cleaned as before.
- **Setup and update checks are off by default.** The setup's extra downloads (yt-dlp, ffmpeg, the video player engine) start unticked, and so do "Check for a newer Utylix now and then" and "Check for a newer yt-dlp now and then" for new installs. Utylix asks the first time a feature needs one of the downloads. Settings you already have are kept.
