PDF editor: edit text like in Word, no more distorted saves, moving pictures keeps their frame

- **Edit text works like a text box.** Click a line and a frame with squares on its right edge appears: drag the square and the words flow to the next line (or back); the frame, the click area and the wrapped result stay as you made them, and a narrowed line keeps its width when you drag it. Words you add to a line wrap by themselves at the right edge. Clicking close to a line (not exactly on the letters) now picks the nearest one.
- **Paragraph tool: choose a block and change its width.** A click on a paragraph or on a centred title (all its lines count as one block) chooses it with a frame; drag the square on its right edge and the words flow again. Double-click changes the words.
- **Line spacing.** New *Spacing* button for an edited line and *Line spacing* choices in the paragraph window.
- **Saving edited text no longer distorts the page.** Saving used to write the whole page again, which turned shaded parts and drawings black and could move things. Now the page's own instructions are left alone: the old text is taken out in place and the new text, shapes, pictures and highlights are added on top.
- **Moving a picture no longer leaves a blank strip.** A picture drawn inside a clipping box (like cards on a sheet) is now moved together with its box.
- **Clicks on empty paper select nothing.** Invisible white boxes and the old place of text you moved or changed can no longer be picked.
- **Save as Word keeps the page looking like the PDF** (colours, drawings, pictures behind; text in place and still editable), with a "Text flows" choice for a plain Word document. Pictures can go inside table cells, and merged cells are chosen whole.
- **Player.** Episodes play in number order (E02 before Episode 3), unfinished downloads (.temp, .part) are skipped, and files that arrive in the folder while you watch are added.
- **Sign from a picture works with see-through PNGs** (it came out blank before). New installs start with the extra downloads and the update checks off.
