PDF editor: measure on drawings, drop pictures on a page, recent files and favourite folders, clean up scans, one job for many PDFs from Explorer

## PDF editor
- **Measure and Area** (Mark up tab). For floor plans and lot plans: set the scale once (the printed scale, like `1:100 m`, or a length you know), then click point after point to measure a distance, or the corners of a shape to measure its area. The answer shows as you move and is written next to the line as normal text (`10.02 m`, `50.20 m²`). Double-click or Enter finishes, Shift keeps a line straight, Esc cancels.
- **Drop pictures on a page.** Drag a picture file onto the editor window and it lands where you let go; move and resize it like any picture.
- **Recent PDFs and favourite folders.** A clock button next to Open lists your last PDFs and the folders you keep (a server folder, a drawings folder). A favourite folder opens the Open window right there and is also listed on its side.
- **Clean up a scan** (Tools menu). A new copy where every page (or this one) is straightened, the paper is white even when the light was uneven, faint text is darker and the dark scanner edges are gone. The original is not changed. The pages become pictures again; use *Make scanned pages searchable (OCR)* afterwards if you need the text.

## Explorer right-click
- Select several PDFs, right-click > *Utylix Editor* > *Do one job to many PDFs…*: they all go into the "Many PDFs at once" window to get a watermark, page numbers, a password, a smaller size and more.
